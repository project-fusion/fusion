# Contributor agreement

By opening a pull request against this repository you agree to the terms below.
There is nothing to sign and no form to fill in: the agreement is the act of
contributing, which is the lightest arrangement that still answers the question a
contributor is entitled to have answered.

Last updated 26 August 2026. **Not reviewed by a lawyer.**

## You keep your copyright, and you license it to everyone

You retain copyright in what you contribute. You grant Fusion Development Group
and every recipient of this project a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
royalty-free, irrevocable license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute and
sublicense your contribution under the MIT license in `LICENSE`.

**A license rather than an assignment**, deliberately. A copyright assignment
takes ownership away from the person who wrote the code, and a project whose
stated test is what happens to its users if Fusion disappears cannot coherently
ask contributors to hand over the thing that would let them carry on without us.
The license is enough for the project to ship; ownership is not ours to need.

**No relicensing on our side.** Fusion will not ship your contribution under
terms more restrictive than MIT. If the project's license ever changes, it
changes forward for new work, and anything you contributed under MIT stays
available under MIT.

## You have the right to contribute it

You confirm that the contribution is your own work, or that you have the right to
submit it under this agreement, and that it does not knowingly infringe anyone
else's rights. If your employer has rights in work you do, you confirm you have
permission or that the employer waives them.

If any part of a contribution is not yours, say so in the pull request and name
its origin and its license. A contribution carrying an unnamed third-party
license is the one thing here that is expensive to unpick later.

## No warranty, and no obligation on either side

You provide contributions as-is, without warranty. We are under no obligation to
merge, keep, or continue to ship any contribution, and may remove or rewrite it.
Contributing creates no employment, partnership or agency relationship, and no
entitlement to compensation.

## AI-assisted contributions

Contributions written with a coding assistant are welcome and are held to the
same standard as any other: you are the contributor, the confirmations above are
yours to make, and "the model wrote it" does not transfer the representation in
the previous section. If a tool reproduced code it did not author, that is the
third-party case above and wants naming.
