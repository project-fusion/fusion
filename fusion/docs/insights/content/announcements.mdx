## What Helix is

A design system that ships as custom elements. Every component is a Lit-based
element with a manifest, so React, Vue, Svelte and whatever comes next consume
the same primitive rather than a wrapper written per framework.

It arrives in two tiers. The headless one carries structure, accessibility,
state and form participation with no stylesheet at all. The styled one extends
it and adds the presentation. You can take the second, or take the first and
bring your own.

## What that gives you

**One brand color in, a full palette out.** The theme engine derives the whole
palette from a seed and refuses one it cannot make readable, so contrast is a
property of the derivation rather than something audited afterwards.

**Components that upgrade in any framework, or none.** A custom element is the
platform's own extension point, so the same component works in the app you have
now and in the one you migrate to.

**A system an agent can read.** Every tag, property and event is in one manifest
at a URL, and every page answers as Markdown. What a teammate reads and what a
model reads are the same document.

**Your repository, your license.** MIT, and the exit test is the point: if you
stopped talking to us tomorrow, you would still have it.

## What we take on around it

Three offers, in the order they usually happen. An **audit** is two weeks at a
fixed price to find out what is worth building. A **design system sprint** is
Helix applied to your product, shipped into your repository rather than drawn in
a design tool. A **conversational build** is development where the specification
is a conversation, for work whose shape is still moving.

[What each involves](/services) is written out, with what you keep at the end of
each one.
