## The stages, and why they are labeled this way

Three labels, used literally. **Active** ships with users. **Building** is real
work with nothing shipped. **Declared** is a committed direction with no work
started.

Separating them is the whole point of the page. The third is the one every
roadmap blurs, and a list that puts all three under one heading is a list where
a plan and a product look the same, which leaves a reader unable to tell which
one they are being offered.

## What is published here

Nothing yet. The stages above are the vocabulary this page will use, and the
entries under them are added as each one becomes true rather than when it
becomes likely. A roadmap that is written ahead of the work is a forecast, and
this page is not one.
