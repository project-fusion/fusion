# Quickstart

Run Cortex entirely on your machine, with no API key. You install the `cortex`
command and two models in Ollama, record three notes, ask a question, trace the
answer back to the notes, and forget a note to watch the answer change.

## Install the command

```bash
npm install --global @fusion.dev/cortex-cli
```

npm installs the binary for your machine: macOS and Linux on Arm and x86-64,
and Windows on x86-64. `cortex` keeps its memory in `cortex.db`, in a `cortex`
folder of your data directory, such as `~/Library/Application Support` on macOS
or `~/.local/share` on Linux.

## Install the models

Install Ollama from [ollama.com](https://ollama.com) and start it. Then pull the
two models `cortex` uses by default:

```bash
ollama pull qwen3:4b
ollama pull nomic-embed-text
```

`qwen3:4b` reads your notes for facts and answers questions. `nomic-embed-text`
embeds each fact, so a question finds a fact that says the same thing in other
words.

## Write three notes

```bash
mkdir notes
cat > notes/monday.md <<'EOF'
The plumber comes on Thursday at 9:00 to fix the kitchen sink.

The spare key is under the blue flower pot by the back door.
EOF
cat > notes/tuesday.md <<'EOF'
The basil on the windowsill needs water every two days.
EOF
cat > notes/wednesday.md <<'EOF'
The plumber moved the visit to Friday at 14:00.
EOF
```

## Record the notes and distill them into facts

`ingest` records each paragraph as an episode, and `--at` dates it.
`consolidate` asks `qwen3:4b` for the facts the new episodes state, and gives
each fact a vector.

```bash
cortex ingest notes/monday.md notes/tuesday.md --at 2026-10-19
cortex consolidate
```

```text
Read 3 episodes and wrote 3 facts.

  fact The plumber comes on Thursday at 9:00 to fix the kitchen sink.
    01a17acc-4abe-…  confidence 1.00, personal, vector ollama/nomic-embed-text
    episode 01a17acb-fd92-…  notes/monday.md, paragraph 1, October 19, 2026
      The plumber comes on Thursday at 9:00 to fix the kitchen sink.

  fact The spare key is under the blue flower pot by the back door.
    01a17acc-4abf-…  confidence 1.00, personal, vector ollama/nomic-embed-text
    episode 01a17acb-fd93-…  notes/monday.md, paragraph 2, October 19, 2026
      The spare key is under the blue flower pot by the back door.

  fact The basil on the windowsill needs water every two days.
    01a17acc-4ac0-…  confidence 1.00, personal, vector ollama/nomic-embed-text
    episode 01a17acb-fd94-…  notes/tuesday.md, paragraph 1, October 19, 2026
      The basil on the windowsill needs water every two days.
```

The output lists each fact with the episode it rests on. The transcripts on
this page come from `qwen3:4b`, which words its facts and answers in its own
way, so yours can read differently. The ids differ on every run, and they are
shortened here.

Then record Wednesday's note and consolidate again:

```bash
cortex ingest notes/wednesday.md --at 2026-10-21
cortex consolidate
```

```text
Read 1 episode and wrote 1 fact.

  fact The plumber moved the visit to Friday at 14:00.
    01a17acc-d581-…  confidence 1.00, personal, vector ollama/nomic-embed-text
    episode 01a17acc-6264-…  notes/wednesday.md, paragraph 1, October 21, 2026
      The plumber moved the visit to Friday at 14:00.

superseded The plumber comes on Thursday at 9:00 to fix the kitchen sink.
  by The plumber moved the visit to Friday at 14:00.
```

The new fact supersedes Monday's. Cortex keeps the old fact, so an answer given
while it was current can still be traced.

## Ask a question

```bash
cortex ask "When does the plumber come?"
```

```text
The plumber comes on Friday at 14:00. This is directly stated in the first fact,
which specifies "the plumber moved the visit to Friday at 14:00." The other facts
about basil and the spare key are irrelevant to the question.

01a17acc-e05f-… from qwen3:4b, on this machine, given 3 facts ranked by
weighted/1 (overlap/2 0.30, vector/1 ollama/nomic-embed-text 0.70), each scoring 0.30 or more
Trace the answer: cortex why 01a17acc-e05f-…
```

Cortex ranks the current facts by the words they share with the question and by
how close their vectors are. The model answered on this machine.

## Trace the answer to your notes

```bash
cortex why
```

```text

  The plumber comes on Friday at 14:00. This is directly stated in the first fact, …
  question When does the plumber come?
  context 01a17acc-d5cc-…  recall/1, 3 facts placed
    fact The plumber moved the visit to Friday at 14:00.
      01a17acc-d581-…  confidence 1.00
      placed: weighted/1 0.61: overlap/2 0.25 weighed 0.30, vector/1 ollama/nomic-embed-text 0.77 weighed 0.70
      episode 01a17acc-6264-…  notes/wednesday.md, paragraph 1, October 21, 2026
        The plumber moved the visit to Friday at 14:00.
    fact The basil on the windowsill needs water every two days.
      01a17acc-4ac0-…  confidence 1.00
      placed: weighted/1 0.38: overlap/2 0.00 weighed 0.30, vector/1 ollama/nomic-embed-text 0.54 weighed 0.70
      episode 01a17acb-fd94-…  notes/tuesday.md, paragraph 1, October 19, 2026
        The basil on the windowsill needs water every two days.
    fact The spare key is under the blue flower pot by the back door.
      01a17acc-4abf-…  confidence 1.00
      placed: weighted/1 0.34: overlap/2 0.00 weighed 0.30, vector/1 ollama/nomic-embed-text 0.49 weighed 0.70
      episode 01a17acb-fd93-…  notes/monday.md, paragraph 2, October 19, 2026
        The spare key is under the blue flower pot by the back door.
```

`why` prints the question, each fact the model was given with the score that
placed it, and the note under each fact. The answer rests on Wednesday's note.

## Forget a note

```bash
cortex forget notes/wednesday.md
```

```text
Forgot 1 episode.
  episode 01a17acc-6264-…  notes/wednesday.md, paragraph 1, October 21, 2026
    The plumber moved the visit to Friday at 14:00.

Removed 1 fact that rested on nothing else.
  fact The plumber moved the visit to Friday at 14:00.

1 fact is current again, since what superseded it is forgotten.
  fact The plumber comes on Thursday at 9:00 to fix the kitchen sink.

Removed 1 answer that drew on them and 8 trace nodes in all.
```

`forget` removes the episode, the fact that rested on it alone, and the answer
that drew on it. Monday's fact is current again. Ask the same question:

```bash
cortex ask "When does the plumber come?"
```

```text
The plumber comes on Thursday at 9:00 to fix the kitchen sink, as stated in the
first fact. The other facts about the basil and the spare key do not provide
information relevant to when the plumber arrives.
```

The answer now rests on Monday's note, and `cortex why` shows it.
