Scroll this page The bar stays put and the page runs under it. What you can see through it is the difference between the two appearances.
Translucency The banner is a pane of material rather than a lid. Content passing beneath it stays visible and goes soft, so the page reads as one surface with a bar floating over it. The cost A backdrop filter makes the header a containing block for anything positioned fixed inside it, so an overlay rendered in the bar is placed against the bar rather than the window. Flat is the default for that reason as much as for the quieter look. The separating rule Both appearances start flat against the top of the page and draw their rule only once something has scrolled underneath, which is why this example is tall enough to scroll.
