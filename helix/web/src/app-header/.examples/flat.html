Scroll this page The bar stays put and the page runs under it. What you cannot see through it is the difference between the two appearances.
Opaque The banner is a lid rather than a pane of material. Content passing beneath it is hidden outright, which is the quieter of the two and the one that keeps the bar legible over anything at all. The default, and why A backdrop filter makes the header a containing block for anything positioned fixed inside it, so an overlay rendered in the bar would be placed against the bar rather than the window. Flat has no filter and so no such surprise. The separating rule Both appearances start flat against the top of the page and draw their rule only once something has scrolled underneath, which is why this example is tall enough to scroll.
