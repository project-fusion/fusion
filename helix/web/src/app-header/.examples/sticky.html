It follows the reader Opt in, because a bar that follows the reader is a decision about the page rather than about the bar. Without it the banner scrolls away with everything else, which is right for a page read once and wrong for an application whose navigation has to stay reachable. It is what the other appearances need A bar that does not stick is only ever over the top of the page, so `material` has nothing to be translucent against and `transparent` never has to become opaque. Both are about what passes underneath, and nothing passes underneath a bar that goes with it. The separating rule It appears once the page has scrolled, which keeps the banner flat against whatever it starts over and lifts it off the content afterwards.
