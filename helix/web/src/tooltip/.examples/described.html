Two ids, and why The tooltip names its trigger with for, which is what puts the two in one tree and lets it listen and position. The trigger names the tooltip back with aria-describedby, which is what a screen reader reads. Both halves are written here rather than one being applied at runtime, so the pairing is in the server's output and holds before any JavaScript has run. The wrapping form Still there, still right where the bubble decorates a control that already says what it is. It cannot describe its trigger, though: the trigger belongs to the page and the bubble is inside this element, and no reference reaches inward across that boundary.
