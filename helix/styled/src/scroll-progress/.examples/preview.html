Scroll this box and the bar above it fills.

A long article reads better with a sense of how much is left, and the bar says it without a number.

It follows the box its for attribute names, and the page when there is none.

It moves under reduced motion too, as a scrollbar does, because it follows the reader's own hand.

On paper it is hidden, since a printed page is not scrolled.

A box with nothing to scroll reads as full, as a page that fits on one screen has been read to its end.

The bar scales rather than resizing, so following a scroll moves no layout.

Place it yourself, such as at the top of a sticky header.
